# Folgers Coffee Jar w/ Ashes

Mian-Mian
2026.08.16

I visited the Museum of Heartbreak in Zagreb, the capital of Croatia, today. It holds objects sent from every corner of the world, each one bearing the weight of a love once felt. They are not especially remarkable in themselves, yet the stories and words attached to them move the heart.

Before a Folgers coffee tin from America, I stood still for a long while. I am now copying down the words of this unnamed woman here, along with a Chinese translation.
 ---

## Folgers Coffee Jar w/ Ashes

1981 – 2020
Seattle, USA

It was a marriage of bliss, intellectual stimulation, compassion and deep love. Supporting each other's happiness guided us on our path.

One of Patrick's favorite movies was <<The Big Lebowski>>. He always said, "When I die, just put my ashes in a Folger's coffee can. No fuss, please."

The end came too soon, too suddenly, completely unexpected. A traumatic loss. He died on my feet, on our 39th anniversary, in an area where I had no connection to emergency services. The numbness, the pain and the shock were as you would expect.

I carry him with me when I travel, sprinkling some of his love wherever I go. Thirty of his close friends are doing the same.

The world needs more of Patrick's romance, love and grace. He will be with me always.

## 一个Folgers咖啡罐 / 骨灰

1981 – 2020
美国，西雅图

那是一段充满幸福、启迪、共情和深爱的婚姻。彼此扶持、互相守护是我们一生的路标。

Patrick最爱的一部电影是《谋杀绿脚趾》。他总说："我死了以后，把我的骨灰装进Folgers咖啡罐里就行。别搞排场，求你了。"

结局来得太快，太突然了，毫无预兆。一次灾难性的失去。他倒在我脚边，在我们的39周年结婚纪念日那天，在一个我连急救电话都打不通的地方。那种麻木、疼痛与震惊，如你所料。

那以后，我旅行时总带着他，所到之处，洒下一点他的爱。他三十位至亲好友，也在做同样的事。

这个世界需要更多Patrick的浪漫、温柔与优雅。他会永远陪着我。

##  江城子·乙卯正月二十日夜记梦

苏轼
宋神宗熙宁八年（1075年）

十年生死两茫茫，不思量，自难忘。

千里孤坟，无处话凄凉。

纵使相逢应不识，尘满面，鬓如霜。


夜来幽梦忽还乡，小轩窗，正梳妆。

相顾无言，惟有泪千行。

料得年年肠断处，明月夜，短松冈。

This famous poem has many translations; here I include the version by my favorite translator, Burton Watson.

## Riverside Town

Su Shi
AD 1075 
—— Burton Watson 译

Ten years, dead and living dim and draw apart.
I don't try to remember,
But forgetting is hard.

Lonely grave a thousand miles off,
Cold thoughts, where can I talk them out?

Even if we met, you wouldn't know me,
Dust on my face,
Hair like frost.


In a dream last night I suddenly got home.
By the small window,
She was combing her hair.

We looked at each other, silent,
A thousand lines of tears.

I suppose it's the place that breaks my heart year after year:
On a moonlit night,
The low pine-covered hill.

one a Song ci, one an exhibition note.
